ABOUT 3D GALLERY — ADDING YOUR PHOTOS

1. Copy your JPG, PNG, AVIF, or WebP files into this folder:
   assets/images/gallery/

2. Open:
   assets/js/gallery-data.js

3. For the item you want to replace, add the exact filename to `file`:
   file: "philadelphia-after-rain.jpg"

4. Edit the same item's information:
   title: the name shown on the photo
   category: Photography, Aquarium, Plants, etc.
   year: the year shown beside the category
   alt: a short visual description for accessibility
   description: the sentence shown when the photo is opened

RECOMMENDED IMAGE SIZE

- Landscape: 1800 × 1350 px (4:3)
- Portrait photos are supported and will be cropped on the sphere.
- JPG/WebP quality around 80–88% keeps the page fast.
- Use simple lowercase filenames without spaces, for example:
  aquarium-closeup-01.webp

If `file` is left empty, the site displays a designed placeholder card.
